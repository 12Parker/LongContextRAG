\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Knowledge conflicts.}
Language models can give priority to memorised knowledge over contradicting context \citep{longpre2021entity}.
They are also readily persuaded by coherent external evidence that contradicts their memory \citep{xie2024chameleon},
and they adopt wrong context over a correct prior to a degree that depends on how strong that prior is
\citep{wu2024clasheval}. \citet{xu2024survey} distinguish context--memory, inter-context and intra-memory conflicts.
We study inter-context conflicts: disagreements among retrieved passages, as in \citet{chen2022rich}, who find that
models tend to rely on a single passage when the evidence conflicts.

\paragraph{Benchmarks with conflicting evidence.}
RAMDocs \citep{wang2025madam} mixes genuine ambiguity (several valid answers), misinformation and noise across
retrieved documents, and builds on AmbigDocs \citep{lee2024ambigdocs}. CONFLICTS \citep{cattan2025conflicts}
annotates search results with a taxonomy of conflict types, each with its own desired behaviour.
WikiContradict \citep{hou2024wikicontradict} pairs Wikipedia passages that genuinely contradict each other.
FaithEval \citep{ming2025faitheval}, ConflictBank \citep{su2024conflictbank} and ConfRAG \citep{yuan2026confrag}
cover related settings. We use RAMDocs because its document labels say which side of a contradiction is the
misinformation, and WikiContradict as a contrast case where neither side is.

\paragraph{Telling the model about conflicts.}
The closest prior work tells the generator that a conflict exists. \citet{cattan2025conflicts} find that prompting a model to
reason about the conflict type helps, and that supplying the gold conflict type helps much more. On WikiContradict,
models acknowledge both sides more often when told the passages may conflict \citep{hou2024wikicontradict}.
Ragability \citep{gross2026ragability} shows the cost of such a hint: it improves conflict identification on
contradictory contexts but hurts it on \emph{consistent} ones, where the hint is false. Our neutral-flag result is
different: the hint is true, the input is contradictory, and accuracy still falls, because the hint does not say
which side to trust. \citet{pan2023risk} test misinformation detection and ``vigilant'' prompting as defences
against LLM-generated misinformation in open-domain QA, and \citet{pan2023attacking} call for QA that is aware of
misinformation. Source signals also matter: models weigh retrieved pages' metadata and appearance
\citep{chiang2024metadata}, and passing source-credibility signals to a retrieval-augmented fact-checker helps resolve
conflicting evidence \citep{ge2025confact}. We separate the \emph{content} of the hint (neutral, directional, removal)
from its \emph{accuracy} (precision, recall, direction), and compare neutral and directional hints head-to-head with
the same detector.

\paragraph{Conflict detection, resolution and poisoning.}
ConflictRAG \citep{wang2026conflictrag} trains a pairwise detector with an LLM fallback and reports pair-level F1
alongside answer gains; its NLI and chain-of-thought baselines resemble two of our detectors. LLMs are imperfect
context validators \citep{gokul2025contradiction}. TCR \citep{ye2026tcr} passes conflict signals through a learned soft
prompt, EvidentialRAG \citep{hossain2026evidentialrag} routes between answering and abstaining, and credibility-aware
generation \citep{pan2024credibility} fine-tunes models to weigh per-passage credibility, a trained analogue of our
directional flags. MADAM-RAG \citep{wang2025madam}, Astute RAG \citep{wang2025astute}, Self-RAG \citep{asai2024selfrag}
and Corrective RAG \citep{yan2024crag} handle unreliable passages without an explicit contradiction detector. A few
injected passages can steer a RAG system \citep{zou2025poisonedrag}; RobustRAG \citep{xiang2024robustrag} isolates
passages for certifiable robustness, and TrustRAG \citep{zhou2025trustrag} filters suspected poisoned ones, the
analogue of our removal format. Our question is orthogonal: given a detector's output, which way of passing it to the
generator helps, and what must the detector get right?
