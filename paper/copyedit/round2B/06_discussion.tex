\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Against misinformation, a neutral flag leaves the model to its answer policy, so in our settings it suits a one-answer
policy with a measured cost for wrong answers \citep{wen2025abstention}. A directional flag is advice generators follow, right or
wrong, as with sycophancy \citep{sharma2024sycophancy}. Such advice pays only above a break-even direction precision, as in
human--AI complementarity \citep{bansal2021accurate,mozannar2020defer}. That threshold calibrates randomised flags on
true pairs. For selective detectors, the frozen follow-rate account predicted unseen cells but not real web pages.
