## Issues for the authors (summaries checked against results.tex)
1. **Bullet 4 is stronger than its source.** Intro: "at a 5\% share of conflict questions the LLM detector's flag lowers accuracy in \prevNegFive\ of \prevN\ settings." Source (results.tex, "When conflicts are rare"): "its flag's estimate is negative in \prevNegFive\ of \prevN\ correlated settings (bootstrap \prevNegFiveCI; significantly in \prevSigNeg, positive in \prevSigPos)". "Lowers accuracy" reads as an effect in 22 settings; the source has a negative point estimate in 22, significant in only 11. It is also an "unregistered re-analysis" (appendix claims table) with no label here.
2. **Bullet 3's causal clause drops the exception and "descriptive".** Intro: "a wrong flag costs more than a right one gains, because generators follow both at similar rates and an accurate generator has more correct answers to lose." Source: "Right- and wrong-flag follow rates overlap (descriptive; Table~\ref{tab:breakeven}) except gpt-6.1-terra's, so $p^\star$ rises mainly because an accurate generator has more to lose." The intro drops "except gpt-6.1-terra's", "descriptive" and "mainly", and attaches the explanation to the wrong-vs-right asymmetry rather than to the rise of $p^\star$. Break-even values are also "exploratory" in the source; the intro gives \beMin\ / \beMax\ unlabelled.
3. **Bullet 3: "account for the effects" vs source "tracks ... mostly through the cell-level rates".** Intro: "the follow rates, combined with what each detector flags, account for the effects (retrospective; ...)". Source: mean error \predEightMae\ with the account vs \predMargMae\ with cell means alone, and "it still misses the Haiku cell". "Retrospective" is kept, but "account for" may overstate what the follow rates add; also no "exploratory".
4. **Bullet 1: "Claude models move less" does not match the source.** Source (results.tex, "Asked for every valid answer"): "both Claude models move the other way" on misinformation share, and Claude "shifts it up (\neuMlAnthropic; ...)" in the pooled model. "Move less" is a different claim from "move the other way".
5. **Bullet 1: "some generators abstain on misinformed and correct answers alike".** Source (H21) is for gpt-4o-mini only: \trSgMiniNeutralMisinfoAbstain\% of misinformed and \trSgMiniNeutralCorrectAbstain\% of correct answers abstain (45 vs 37). "Alike" is a fair gloss, but the measurement is one generator, and "some generators" is backed by the accuracy costs (H15, H19), not by the abstention breakdown.
6. **Bullet 2: equivalence drops "exploratory".** Intro: "on average it is equivalent to an answer key that names the wrong answer (pooled over \PoolTwentyThreeK\ comparisons, which vary individually)". Source labels the pooled TOST "exploratory" and says equivalence holds in only \hTwentyThreeEqN\ of \hTwentyThreeK\ individually. "Which vary individually" is a softer version of that caveat.
7. **Bullet 3 holds a finding its head does not cover.** "The detector's verdict as an answer key matches its flag (H30); the detector model's own answer as a hint does worse (H27)." sits under "A directional flag pays only above a break-even precision." It is the abstract's separate claim ("What a detector contributes is its verdict"). I did not move it (moving a contribution claim between heads felt beyond Round A); the authors may want it as its own bullet, which would add a head's worth of words. Also, H30's equivalence is pooled-only (per-cell max \hThirtyAMax\ > 3) and that pooled TOST is unregistered.
8. **Controls sentence mislabels the verdict as a hint.** "Controls separate what a flag conveys: forced choice, an answer key, randomised direction, the detector's verdict and its model's own answer as hints, and six detectors crossed with six generators." Per the glossary the verdict is passed as an answer key (H30, *verdict key*); only the model's own answer is a hint (H27). Fixing it ("the detector's verdict as an answer key and its model's own answer as a hint") adds about 4 words. Forced choice and the crossed design are also not controls in the method's sense (method.tex Controls = pick, no pairs, answer key).
9. **The addenda sentence is still about 45 words.** "Eight addenda (H10--H31), each committed before its runs, extended the study to ConflictBank ... Splitting it further needs extra words. Also "whose misleading side is real Reddit posts" has a number mismatch (side ... is ... posts).
